# The Zoth Ecosystem

### A Privacy-First Stablecoin Neobank for the Global South and the Agentic Economy

Zoth is building a privacy-first stablecoin neobank, an integrated financial infrastructure designed to unify yield, payments, compliance, and security into a single institution-grade platform.

Institutional adoption of stablecoin-based banking services remains limited. Existing public ledger infrastructure exposes suppliers, payment corridors, transaction volumes, and treasury activity to competitors, making privacy not just a technical concern, but a fundamental business requirement. The challenge is even more acute across the Global South, where local currency volatility compounds the problem and institutions remain underserved by traditional banking infrastructure that is too slow, too rigid, and poorly suited to their scale and pace of growth.

Zoth is purpose-built to close this gap. As a privacy-first stablecoin neobank, Zoth is built on enterprise-grade privacy rails that protect payment flows, counterparties, and corridor activity from public exposure, while remaining fully accessible to the institutions, enterprises, and financial systems driving the next wave of Global South growth. By combining the accessibility of a neobank with enterprise-grade security, regulated infrastructure, and compliant-by-design architecture, Zoth delivers a new financial layer for institutions operating across borders.

The ecosystem is built around five core offerings:

* Earn: Access to sustainable, transparent yield through diversified RWA and DeFi strategies, delivered through a fully auditable, compliant, and institution-ready framework.
* Send: Instant, near-zero-cost borderless payments through privacy-preserving infrastructure optimized for high-frequency, cross-border financial flows.
* Spend (Coming Soon): Bridge on-chain balances with real-world spending through seamless payment access across global commerce environments.
* Agentic: A machine-native financial layer with infrastructure that enables AI agents and autonomous systems to transact, coordinate, and settle capital securely at scale.
* Zocta Points: The universal loyalty and coordination layer, connecting and incentivizing participation across the ecosystem for users, businesses, and agents alike.

Zoth provides the institution-grade rails for a new financial internet, one that is privacy-first, compliant by design, and built for both institutional operators and the emerging agentic economy. From enterprise treasury and cross-border settlement to machine-speed financial coordination, Zoth enables capital to move, grow, and settle securely without borders.
