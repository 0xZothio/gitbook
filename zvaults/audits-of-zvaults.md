# Audits of zVaults

The contracts are audited by independent blockchain security firms before deployment and on an ongoing basis as the codebase evolves. Each review examines contract logic, access controls, and integration points, and the findings are tracked to resolution rather than treated as a one-time gate. This provides continuous external assurance over the protocol's integrity, separate from the internal review that governs upgrades.

Reports are published at [https://github.com/0xZothio/zVault/blob/dev/audits/ENTERSOFT.pdf](https://github.com/0xZothio/zVault/blob/dev/audits/ENTERSOFT.pdf).
