# Layer 1: Stablecoin NeoBank Apps

The first layer is the user-facing surface of the Zoth ecosystem, delivering a full-stack stablecoin neobank experience across three core product verticals.

**Earn** gives businesses and individuals access to sustainable yield through zVaults and curated partner products, bringing institutional-grade savings and investment infrastructure within reach of anyone, anywhere.

**Send** enables instant, low-cost cross-border payments with seamless on-ramp and off-ramp capabilities, making global money movement as simple and accessible as a local transfer.

**Spend** brings on-chain balances into the real world through Zoth Cards and Zoth Corporate Cards, giving users direct spending access globally. (Coming Soon)

Together, these three verticals form a complete financial experience: save, move, and spend, built on stablecoin rails and designed for the Global South and beyond.
