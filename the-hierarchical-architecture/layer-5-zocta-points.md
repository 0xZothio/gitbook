# Layer 5: Zocta Points

The fifth layer functions as the unifying incentive and coordination engine of the Zoth ecosystem, aligning participation across individuals, businesses, institutions, and autonomous systems through a single, interoperable loyalty layer.

At its core, Zocta Points convert ecosystem activity into measurable value. Meaningful actions across the platform whether earning yield, sending payments, spending through cards, deploying agents, or contributing to ecosystem growth can be recognized and rewarded within one unified points framework.

This layer is designed to go beyond traditional rewards systems. Zocta Points operate as a programmable loyalty primitive, enabling dynamic incentive structures that can be tailored across products, partners, user segments, and financial use cases. This allows Zoth to drive targeted growth, strengthen engagement, and align long-term participation across both retail and institution-grade financial activity.

For individuals, Zocta Points create a continuous reward loop where everyday financial activity can translate into tangible ecosystem value. For businesses and institutions, they act as a strategic growth lever, supporting embedded incentives, user acquisition, retention, and ecosystem participation directly within financial flows. For autonomous systems and agentic actors, Zocta Points add an incentive layer that can be integrated into programmable workflows, allowing agents to optimize for rewards while executing transactions and financial tasks within policy and compliance controls.

Seamlessly integrated across all five layers, Zocta Points connect the Zoth stack into a cohesive economic system, ensuring that participation across payments, yield, spend, and agentic activity contributes to broader ecosystem growth, coordination, and long-term alignment.

By acting as the universal incentive layer, Layer 5 helps transform Zoth from a collection of financial products into a connected, self-reinforcing financial ecosystem.
