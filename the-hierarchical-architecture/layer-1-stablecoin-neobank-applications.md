# Layer 1: Stablecoin Neobank Applications

The first layer is the application layer of the Zoth ecosystem, delivering a privacy-first stablecoin neobank experience across three core product verticals.

Earn provides institutions, enterprises, and eligible participants with access to sustainable yield through zVaults and curated partner products, extending institution-grade savings and treasury infrastructure through compliant and transparent financial rails.

Send enables privacy-preserving cross-border payments with integrated on-ramp and off-ramp infrastructure, making global money movement faster, more efficient, and operationally seamless across complex corridors.

Spend brings on-chain balances into real-world payment environments through Zoth Cards and Zoth Corporate Cards, enabling secure and compliant global spend access. (Coming Soon)

Together, these three verticals form a unified financial experience: earn, move, and spend capital through private, compliant, and programmable stablecoin infrastructure built for institutions, enterprises, and the evolving agentic economy.
