# The Hierarchical Architecture

Zoth is built on a unique five-layer architecture that bridges institutional-grade infrastructure with stablecoin-native financial applications. This stack is engineered to provide enterprises, global businesses, and agents with a seamless, compliant gateway to the Agentic Economy, while extending high-tier financial access to individuals across the Global South.

Each layer is purpose-built and deeply integrated, creating a unified platform where compliance, security, yield, and programmability operate together rather than in isolation.

#### Layer 1: Stablecoin NeoBank Apps

Forms the user-facing surface of the ecosystem, delivering three core product verticals: Earn, through zVaults and curated partner products; Send, enabling cross-border payments, on-ramp, and off-ramp; and Spend, through Zoth Cards and Zoth Corporate Cards.

#### Layer 2: Agentic Economy Solutions

Powers the machine-native financial layer, featuring Zocta AI Agents built directly into the platform, tools to build and deploy custom agents, and the broader Agentic AI Economy Infrastructure that enables autonomous systems to transact, settle, and operate financially at scale.

#### Layer 3: Institutional Grade Tech Infrastructure

Underpins the entire stack with AI-powered risk management, multi-layer security including AI-powered monitoring and MPC, a privacy protection framework, payment infrastructure, and end-to-end asset tokenization capabilities.

#### Layer 4: Strong Regulatory Moats

Provides the compliance foundation across jurisdictions, encompassing payment orchestration infrastructure, a regulated fund structure, payment licenses, Money Transmitter Licenses across 50 US states, New York BitLicense, NYDFS Trust Company Charter, and FinCEN MSB Registration, with regulatory presence spanning QFC, Cayman Islands, ADGM, and VARA.

#### Layer 5: Zocta Points

Serves as the universal loyalty layer, connecting and incentivizing participation across the entire ecosystem for users, businesses, and agents alike.
