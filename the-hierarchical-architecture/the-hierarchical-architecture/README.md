# The Hierarchical Architecture

### A Unique Five-Layer Integrated Financial Stack

Zoth is built on a five-layer architecture that connects institution-grade infrastructure with stablecoin-native financial applications. This stack is designed to provide institutions, enterprises, and autonomous systems with a private, compliant, and programmable financial layer for cross-border treasury, payments, and machine-native finance.

Each layer is purpose-built and deeply integrated, creating a unified system where privacy, compliance, security, yield, and programmability work together as a single financial stack rather than as fragmented point solutions.

**Layer 1:** Stablecoin Neobank Applications is the application layer of the ecosystem, delivering three core product verticals: Earn, through zVaults and curated partner products; Send, enabling privacy-preserving cross-border payments, on-ramp, and off-ramp infrastructure; and Spend, through Zoth Cards and Zoth Corporate Cards.

**Layer 2:** Agentic Economy Solutions powers the machine-native financial economy, including Zoth's agentic payment infrastructure, Zocta AI Agents integrated directly into the platform, tools to build and deploy custom agents, and the broader infrastructure that enables autonomous systems to transact, settle, and coordinate capital at scale within policy and under compliance controls.

**Layer 3:** Institution-Grade Technology Infrastructure forms the technical core of the stack, with AI-powered risk management, multi-layer security architecture including AI-driven monitoring and MPC, privacy-preserving financial infrastructure, enterprise payment systems, and end-to-end asset tokenization capabilities.

**Layer 4:** Regulatory and Compliance Infrastructure provides the regulatory foundation across jurisdictions, encompassing payment orchestration infrastructure and regulated fund structures. Licenses include Money Transmitter Licenses across 50 US states, New York BitLicense, NYDFS Trust Company Charter, and FinCEN MSB Registration, alongside regulatory presence across QFC, Cayman Islands, ADGM, and VARA.

**Layer 5:** Zocta Points serves as the universal loyalty and coordination layer, connecting and incentivizing participation across the ecosystem for enterprises, institutions, and autonomous systems including agentic actors.
